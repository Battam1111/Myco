# Layer B — Generative Fragments (chengyu / 短诗)

> **What this is**: 60 short fragments of classical-leaning Chinese — chengyu, couplets, or single images in the Tao Te Ching mode. They are *eyes* the cultivator-cultivar pair use to perceive situations the Layer A cards' formulations cannot anticipate. They are NOT specs. They are NEVER CI-validated.
>
> **How to read**: end-to-end, in one sitting if possible. Let the fragments accumulate cross-resonances in your mind. A vague fragment is disambiguated by a neighbor. Returning readers may sample.
>
> **Authority**: every fragment derives from at least one Layer A card. The card's deposit + formulation is the *spec*; the fragment is the *eye*. Both are needed; they do different work.
>
> **Lifecycle**: per META §4.5. When a fragment is invoked in a real decision, the decision + outcome attaches as a `Commentary` line on the fragment. The fragment text never changes; commentary accumulates. Dormant fragments (6+ months without invocation) are retirement candidates; cultivator decides. Retired fragments are marked `[DORMANT]`, never deleted.

## §13. Reading discipline

These 60 fragments are the **initial corpus**. New fragments are added per META §7.2 (Cultivator proposes; Cultivator confirms; M-anchor-5 anchored since Layer B is part of L0).

Fragments retire to `[DORMANT]` per META §4.3 — never deleted. Future readers may rediscover insight in a dormant fragment.

**Cross-resonance is the point.** B003 (永恒吞噬) and B040 (飢即活) and B004 (飢而後動) and B033 (擇而食非塞而食) are all about absorption — but each carries a different angle, and reading them together produces what no single one offers. Read for the resonance, not for the catalogue.

**No explanation rule** (META §4.1): if a fragment requires explanation beyond its English gloss, it has failed at its job. Explanation belongs in Layer A cards (operational definition, illustrations, misreadings). Layer B is for the *eye*, not the *spec*.
